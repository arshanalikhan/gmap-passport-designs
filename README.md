# GMAP Material Passport: landscape designs

Live picker: https://arshanalikhan.github.io/gmap-passport-designs/

- `index.html`: picker page with Level 2 and Level 4 passports (with Degradation). Lets you change MCI, Degradation, header and name-band colours, the header jaali pattern and strength, and the range bar style. ★ Recommended restores the chosen look.
- `L2_passport.html`, `L4_passport.html`: the two passport cards on their own, 1120×630, print-ready.

Plain HTML/CSS. There is no build step.
