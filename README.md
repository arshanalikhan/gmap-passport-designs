# GMAP Material Passport: landscape designs

Live picker: https://arshanalikhan.github.io/gmap-passport-designs/

- `index.html`: picker page with Level 2, Level 3 and Level 4 passports (with Degradation). Lets you change MCI, Degradation, header and name-band colours, the header jaali pattern and strength, and the range bar style. ★ Recommended restores the chosen look.
- `L2_passport.html`, `L3_passport.html`, `L4_passport.html`: the three passport cards on their own, 1120×630, print-ready. L2 and L3 show ranges across the levels below them; L4 shows one product's exact values.

- `L4_rebar_location_lines.html`, `L4_rebar_climate_lines.html`: worst-case Degradation examples for reinforcement steel (13 positions = building part × location): one line per position with climate zones C1–C6 on it, or one line per climate zone with the 13 positions on it.

Plain HTML/CSS. There is no build step.
